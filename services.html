Services

Simple process. Powerful results.

I work with small businesses to create custom websites that attract customers and drive real results. Here's what I offer.

01

Website Design & Development

I build custom-designed websites from scratch, tailored specifically to your brand, your audience, and your business goals. No templates. No shortcuts. Just clean, fast, professional code and design.

Every design decision is intentional. From layout to color to typography, everything is optimized to communicate trust, build credibility, and convert visitors into leads.

What's Included:

  • Custom website design (no templates)
  • Professional HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Mobile-responsive layout
  • Contact forms and email integration
  • Basic SEO optimization
  • SSL security and hosting setup
02

Mobile-Responsive Design

Most of your customers browse on phones. Your website needs to look perfect on every device—smartphone, tablet, laptop. I build every site mobile-first, so it works flawlessly everywhere.

Fast loading times, touch-friendly navigation, and readable text on small screens aren't optional—they're essential. Your site will perform beautifully whether someone visits from their phone on a construction site or their desktop at home.

What's Included:

  • Mobile-first responsive design
  • Touch-optimized buttons and menus
  • Fast page load speeds
  • Optimized images and assets
  • Cross-browser compatibility testing
  • Performance monitoring and optimization
03

Ongoing Support & Maintenance

Your website doesn't stay the same. Seasons change, services evolve, and technology updates. I offer monthly maintenance plans so your site stays secure, fast, and relevant.

No surprises. No emergency calls. Just regular updates, security patches, performance monitoring, and the technical work you don't want to handle. You focus on your business; I handle the tech.

What's Included:

  • Monthly security updates
  • Content and page updates
  • Performance monitoring
  • Backup and disaster recovery
  • Technical support via email
  • Uptime monitoring and alerts
